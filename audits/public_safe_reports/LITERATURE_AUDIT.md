# Q438 literature and target check

**27 November 2026. High confidence on source, moderate confidence on current literature coverage.** The candidate answers the restricted minimum-cardinality hardness question. It separates verification of arbitrary supplied measurements from the empty-dataset case. A bounded search found no earlier theorem with this nondegenerate scope.

- Ye, Amin and Özdağlar, *Learning Decision-Sufficient Representations for Linear Optimization*, PMLR 336, 6924–6975, ask in §7, p. 6935, whether exact minimum-size hardness persists for nondegenerate standard-form feasible polytopes. The latest arXiv v2 formulates this in §8. Assumption 12 means each vertex has exactly as many positive coordinates as the row rank. Definition 1 asks for recovery of the full optimizer set for every cost from one dataset. Definition 6 asks a pointwise question using a common optimizer within a fiber. The pinned catalog specifies a full-dimensional open convex rational H-prior. The proceedings also ask about approximate cardinality and noisy queries. Adaptive pointwise complexity is Q439. [Proceedings](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v336/ye26a.html) · [Published PDF, p. 6935](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mlresearch/v336/main/assets/ye26a/ye26a.pdf#page=12) · [arXiv v2, §8](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.18551v2#S8) · [Pinned Q438](https://github.com/10m-u/open-problems-catalog/blob/ca8d4d44ba3db527f19f28928c240168f545ecfe/problems/mathematics/optimization-operations-research.md#q438)
- Bennouna, Bennouna, Amin and Özdağlar establish the subspace characterization for open convex priors. Their 2025 preprint Corollary 1 characterizes sufficiency by containment of the direction span generated by reachable optimal vertices. Ye–Amin–Özdağlar Corollary 4 credits this result and uses the dimension as the minimum cardinality. The mixed-integer construction does not give a worst-case polynomial algorithm. [2025 preprint, §§3.2–3.3](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.21692#S3) · [2026 working paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.15365) · [Published Corollary 4](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mlresearch/v336/main/assets/ye26a/ye26a.pdf#page=5)
- Ley and Merkert, *Solution methods for partial inverse combinatorial optimization problems in which weights can only be increased*, J. Global Optim. 93 (2025), 263–298, Theorem 3.1 proves NP-completeness on a DAG with one required arc and no forbidden arcs. Their variable and clause chains and shortcut arcs supply the baseline construction. Theorem 5 and Appendix A.1 of Ye–Amin–Özdağlar adapt it to unrestricted decision dimension, also with an open prior. These constructions do not establish the nondegeneracy promise. [Ley–Merkert](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10898-025-01529-x) · [Author version Appendix A.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.18551v2#A1)
- Theorem 9 of Ye–Amin–Özdağlar treats pointwise verification. Theorem 10 makes empty-dataset global verification coNP-hard without nondegeneracy, and Corollary 11 transfers hardness to the unrestricted global minimum. Their §5 gives a polynomial pointwise algorithm under Assumption 12. Under that promise the current work makes empty-dataset verification polynomial but verification of an arbitrary supplied dataset coNP-complete under many-one reductions. [Published §§4–5](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mlresearch/v336/main/assets/ye26a/ye26a.pdf#page=5)
- Ye and Bennouna, *Solving Linear and Integer Programs Faster Using Data*, arXiv v3 dated 16 November 2026, give exact affine reformulations from sufficient subspaces and sample-based guarantees without nondegeneracy. Their §§1 and 3.2 invoke earlier unrestricted hardness and do not settle the promised minimum global problem. [Primary preprint](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.25635v3#S3)

The new restriction combines exact vertex nondegeneracy and a full-dimensional open cost prior with a single capacity that exposes a direction exchanging uncapped parallel arc flows. The two-prior argument gives polynomial-time Turing NP-hardness for exact dimension and constructing a minimum dataset. It does not give many-one NP-hardness for the dimension-threshold problem or establish approximation hardness. No priority claim is made.
