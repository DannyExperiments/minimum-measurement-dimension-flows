# Provenance

The restricted-complexity question is in Ye, Amin and Özdağlar’s COLT proceedings, §7, p. 6935, under Assumption 12. Their latest arXiv v2 gives it in §8. The public catalog statement is pinned to revision ca8d4d44ba3db527f19f28928c240168f545ecfe. [Proceedings](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v336/ye26a.html) · [Pinned Q438](https://github.com/10m-u/open-problems-catalog/blob/ca8d4d44ba3db527f19f28928c240168f545ecfe/problems/mathematics/optimization-operations-research.md#q438)

The manuscript expands the recovered nondegenerate-case proof. It credits Bennouna et al. for the global subspace characterization and Ley and Merkert for the inverse shortest-path baseline. Ye, Amin and Özdağlar already give unrestricted hardness. The new construction uses tiny internal demands, one capacity and two cost priors whose projected optimal sets agree.
